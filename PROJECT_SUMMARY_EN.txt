MC Ecosystem Adaptation Engineer is an intelligent assistance system designed for Minecraft mod players. Its core capabilities include mod environment diagnosis, version recommendation, crash analysis and fix, and automatic repair. It supports all mainstream mod loaders including NeoForge, Forge, Fabric, and Quilt, and covers popular mod ecosystems such as Create, Fun series, IndustrialCraft, Applied Energistics, and many more.
